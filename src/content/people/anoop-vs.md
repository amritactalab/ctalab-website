Dr. Anoop V. S. is the Director of the Amrita Computational Text Analysis Lab (AmritaCTALab) and an Assistant Professor (Level 12) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, School of Computing, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Amritapuri.

His primary research interests span Applied Natural Language Processing (SocialNLP and IndicNLP), Computational Text Analysis, Semantic Text Kernels, Biomedical and Legal AI, and explainable deep learning for unstructured textual data. He earned his Ph.D. and M.Phil. from the Faculty of Technology, Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT), and has previously held academic and research positions at Digital University Kerala, Kerala Blockchain Academy (IIITM-K), and Rajagiri College of Social Sciences.

He serves as a Research Topic Editor for *Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems* and *Frontiers in Blockchain*, and has edited multiple research volumes published by CRC Press, Taylor & Francis.
